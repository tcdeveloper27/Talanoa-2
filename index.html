Modelling

Use the board yourself while you talk with him: the best way to teach it. While modelling is on, your taps are said but not counted, and the brown bar turns purple. 👆 then a picture makes it glow for a moment without saying it (a hint). It switches itself off after 10 minutes without a tap.
